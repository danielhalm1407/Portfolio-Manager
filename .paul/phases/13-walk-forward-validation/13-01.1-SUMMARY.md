## The measurement

One `ProtectivePutRule` v2 configuration over leading slices of the real window — the same
measurement `13-01-IMPLEMENTATION-CONTEXT.md` section 5 made, so the fix is proven against the
measurement that found the bug.

**THE SINGLE-RUN CONSTANT, MEASURED: 5,201 bars in 6.30 s (1.21 ms/bar).** 13-01's AC-6 records
this and 13-02's AC-1 multiplies it. Against the superseded ~6.5 min projection that is ~62x.

**Use the measured 6.3 s, not a projection from the 2,000-bar slice.** Projecting forward from
0.36 ms/bar gives 1.9 s and reality is 3.3x that, because a residual O(positions) term survives —
see Concerns.

## Decisions Made

| Decision | Rationale | Impact |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| The fix is a `StateLedger` SUBCLASS via `simulator.py`'s existing `ledger=` argument | `13-01-IMPLEMENTATION-CONTEXT.md` section 6 listed four candidate fixes, concluded all four touch `Book`/`Ledger`/`Simulator`, and therefore that an amendment was required. There is a fifth option it did not consider, and it touches none of them | No boundary amendment needed. Byte-identity for `rebalance_study` / `drawdown_rotation_sim` / `overlay_runs` is structural — they pass no `ledger=`, so their code path does not change — rather than something that had to be hashed and argued |
| Per-leg ledger columns are dropped outright | They are the entire quadratic term. The per-leg story is carried by `sim.blotter()` (~164 fills over 20 years, O(1) each) and `rule.history` | The narrow frame **cannot explain its own equity path**. Every caller must persist all three artefacts; stated in the module header, in 13-01's amended AC-6 and in 13-02's AC-2 |
| `df` is a CACHED property, not a rebuilt one | `simulator.run()` reads `ledger.df` once and then writes `equity_peak` / `drawdown` / `turnover_cum` onto it (`simulator.py:116-127`). A per-access rebuild would hand back a fresh object and those columns would vanish silently | Pinned by `test_df_returns_the_same_object_and_caller_columns_survive` — the single easiest thing to break with a lazy property |
| `_put_row` overridden rather than only `record_book` | Routes the inherited single-asset `record()` path through the same `{ts: row}` store | `record()` keeps working instead of silently mutating a cached frame `_rows` knows nothing about. Smaller override surface too |
| `classify_option_events` derives open-vs-close from the RUNNING POSITION, not from `side` | A long put is bought to open and sold to close; a collar's short call is sold to open and bought to close. `side` classifies the two structures in opposite directions | One definition that works for every structure. Known ambiguity documented: a monetise-and-reopen on the SAME bar is indistinguishable from a roll in the blotter alone |
| `Book` dead-leg retirement NOT done | It is candidate fix 2 in 13-01's context section 6, it changes `book.symbols` semantics and therefore everything iterating the book, and it was not needed to make a run affordable | Routed to 13-01 as finding 2. 6.3 s is already affordable, so this is an optimisation with real regression risk, not a blocker |

> **UPDATE 2026-09-21.** Findings 1 and 2 have since been RESOLVED under 13-01 — see the `RESOLVED`
> blocks in [13-01-PLAN.md](13-01-PLAN.md). Finding 1 went a third way, neither of the two readings
> recorded below: the drawdown REFERENCE resets on reopen (`07615c7`), and `_gate_open()` is
> unchanged. Finding 2 became a skip of flat positions rather than the retirement this summary
> anticipated (`ff2caed`), because retiring a `Position` would delete the realised P&L it booked.
> Finding 3 was resolved on 2026-09-22 and turned out to be larger than a missing panel: the
> history the figure reads from had no row on the monetise bar or on any FLAT bar, which is why
> the thrash took fifteen tooltips to find. Finding 4 was RETIRED at the user's direction and
> replaced by a value-as-multiple-of-premium panel. The diagnosis below is kept verbatim as the
> record of what was measured on the day.

**1. The monetise / reopen thrash — the significant one.** Rung 3c monetises and reopens on
consecutive bars, repeatedly:

```
2008-05-07  monetise  drawdown 0.1081  iv 0.1826
2008-05-08  reopen    SPY P 125.24     iv 0.1778
2008-05-09  monetise  drawdown 0.1121  iv 0.1810
```

Over the full history: **281 monetisations against 48 rolls.** `_gate_open()` tests implied vol
against `reentry_iv` and nothing else — it never tests whether the monetise trigger is still
firing. Mid-2008 IV sat at 0.16-0.18, so the gate opened the next bar, the rule re-struck into a
market still >10% below its peak, and the trigger fired again. `_peak` does not reset on a
monetise, so the drawdown condition stays true for as long as the market is below its old peak.
Both gates demonstrably work (the 2009-03-03 reopen fires at exactly 126 flat bars), so this is
either the wrong `reentry_iv` level — 0.18 is near SPY's unconditional median IV, so it is barely a
gate at all — or a missing DRAWDOWN-RECOVERED condition. The plan says test the cheap reading first.

**2. The residual timing term is in `Book`, not the ledger.** `Book` (live state: a dict of
`Position`s) and `StateLedger` (the per-bar recording) are different objects; 13-01.1 narrowed the
recording only. `Book.position()` never removes a `Position`, so `Book.unrealised()` and
`Book.gross_exposure()` each walk ~82 dead legs on every one of 5,201 bars. That is the whole of
the 0.36 → 1.21 ms/bar gap between the 2,000-bar slice and the full history.

**3. The IV series needs a second panel on the probe figure.** The gate is a comparison between a
LEVEL and a SERIES, and a comparison is a picture, not a tooltip. Finding 1 was diagnosed by
reading fifteen hover boxes one at a time; `reentry_iv` drawn across the real IV path would have
shown "the gate is nearly always open" at a glance.

**4. Pricing and mechanics are still only spot-checked on real data.** The numbers look sane but
"looks sane" is not the standard this phase set elsewhere. One bar of rung 3b's strikes and premia
should be hand-checked against a direct `synthetic_iv_surface` + `black_scholes_put` call, to rule
out an off-by-one in the moneyness reference or the tenor.
