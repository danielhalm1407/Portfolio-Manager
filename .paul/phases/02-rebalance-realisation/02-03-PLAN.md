<objective>
## Goal

Every trade in the study is a hoverable marker that states its own rationale in prose, and the
`run_books` call chain is documented — what each stage produces and, explicitly, what it does not.

## Purpose

02-02 makes the rationale exist. This plan makes it *legible*. A table of realised P&L by ticker
says the hedge banked money; it does not show the individual decision that banked it. A scatter of
fills, coloured by side and hovering its own reason, does — and it is the artefact that makes the
"sold because it fell less, and booked a loss doing it" case visible rather than merely recorded.

The documentation half closes a separate gap: reading `study.run_books(...)` in the research script
tells you nothing about the four layers underneath it, and `rebalance_study.py` has no companion map
of the kind the repo's targeted-read convention expects.

## Output

A `fig_trades` figure function, a new study cell, a per-method walkthrough of `rebalance_study.py`,
and a rationale section in the research write-up.
</objective>
